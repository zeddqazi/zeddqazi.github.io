Nano Quiz Clash

A real-time science quiz for 2–8 players, each on their own phone or laptop.

How to play
  1. One person taps Host a game and shares the 4-letter room code.
  2. Everyone else opens this page on their own device, enters the code and a nickname, and taps Join.
  3. The host starts when 2–8 players are in. There are 8 multiple-choice questions on nanoscience and biotech, with 15 seconds each.
  4. A correct answer scores 500 points, plus up to 500 bonus points for speed. A wrong answer or no answer scores 0. You can't change an answer once it's locked in.
  5. After every question everyone sees the right answer and the leaderboard. The highest total after question 8 wins. Ties share the place.
